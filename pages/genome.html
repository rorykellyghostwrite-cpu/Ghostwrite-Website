New TCG in Development

What Is Genome: Apex Shifting?

Genome: Apex Shifting is an upcoming indie trading card game built around evolution, ecology and competitive strategy. Players command entire ecosystems rather than relying on a single species, faction or hero.

Each player develops an ecosystem filled with creatures, predators, prey, environmental effects and evolutionary possibilities. Genes can strengthen existing organisms. Afflictions can destabilize an opposing ecosystem. Climate cards can transform the battlefield. Hybrid cards can combine evolutionary paths into powerful Apex organisms.

The primary objective is to reduce your opponent's Dominance to zero. How you accomplish that depends on the ecosystem you build and the evolutionary strategy you choose.

The World Behind the Cards

What Is the Story of Genome?

Before mankind ruled the Earth, the gods ruled mankind.

Long before recorded history, the gods did not wage war against one another directly. Instead, they chose human champions. Entire civilizations became extensions of divine ambition.

Odin rallied the Vikings. Zeus commanded the Greeks. Tyr and Ares each forged warriors in their own image. Ra watched over Egypt. Amaterasu guided Japan. Every pantheon cultivated its people, believing its followers would one day establish the dominant civilization of the world.

Every conquest, every crusade and every empire became another move in a game played by immortal hands.

Why Did the Gods Abandon Humanity?

Time changed mankind.

Humanity grew independent. Faith weakened. Nations stopped belonging to gods and began belonging to themselves. People no longer marched because Odin commanded them. They marched because kings demanded it. They no longer sacrificed for Zeus. They questioned him.

The gods had not lost their power.

They had lost their influence.

What Was Loki's Proposal?

It was Loki who first spoke the idea aloud.

If humanity had become too unpredictable, why continue relying upon it?

Why wage wars through creatures capable of doubt, rebellion and free will when evolution itself could become the battlefield?

The other gods laughed.

Then they listened.

What Is the Apex Shift?

The pantheons abandoned humanity as their chosen armies and turned their attention toward life itself.

Ecosystems became kingdoms. Evolution became a weapon. Predators were reshaped. Species were rewritten. Every mutation was intentional. Every adaptation served a god.

The old wars never ended.

They simply found new soldiers.

Competitive Tabletop Strategy

How Do You Play Genome: Apex Shifting?

Players begin with an ecosystem and a strategy for survival. Creatures enter the battlefield, interact with their environment and create opportunities for attack, defense, growth and adaptation.

Genes enhance creatures with new abilities. Afflictions weaken or infect opposing organisms. Climate effects alter the conditions of play. Hybrid Apex cards represent impossible evolutionary leaps capable of changing an entire match.

Players reduce opposing Dominance through combat, evolutionary advantages, environmental disruption, disease, resource pressure and ecosystem control.

No two games develop in exactly the same way because every ecosystem changes according to the creatures, Genes, Afflictions, Climate effects and tactical decisions played during the match.

What Makes Genome Different From Other TCGs?

Genome treats the ecosystem itself as part of the battlefield. Creatures are not isolated units waiting to attack on command. They exist within a larger network of predators, prey, environmental pressures and evolutionary possibilities.

Players are encouraged to build strategies around ecology, adaptation and interaction. A fallen creature may feed another organism. A Gene may turn an overlooked species into a major threat. An Affliction may spread through an opposing ecosystem. A Climate shift may transform a reliable strategy into a desperate fight for survival.

This creates a strategy card game where the battlefield changes continuously and survival depends on more than simply playing the largest creature.

Is Genome a TCG, CCG or Board Game?

Genome: Apex Shifting is designed as a competitive tabletop trading card game. It belongs to the same broad family of games commonly described as TCGs, collectible card games, customizable card games, deck-building games and strategic tabletop card games.

Players searching for new board games may also find Genome through its tabletop strategy, ecosystem management, asymmetrical starter decks and replayable competitive gameplay. Unlike a traditional board game, however, the primary components and strategies are built around cards, decks and evolving combinations.

Why Does Evolution Matter in Genome?

Evolution is not merely part of the artwork or story. It drives the mechanics of the game.

Every creature can be enhanced through Genes. Every Gene can change how that organism interacts with combat, resources, predators, prey, Climate or Afflictions. Hybrid Apex organisms represent radical evolutionary transformations capable of redefining the battlefield.

Every ecosystem evolves differently. Every match produces a new chain of adaptations. Every battle tells a different story.

Is Genome: Apex Shifting Available to Play?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Genome: Apex Shifting a new trading card game?

Yes. Genome is an original indie trading card game currently in alpha development. It combines competitive card play with ecosystems, creature evolution, environmental strategy and genetic enhancement.

When will Genome: Apex Shifting be released?

No public release date has been announced. The game is currently undergoing alpha testing, mechanic refinement and deck balancing. Development updates will be shared through the GhostWrite Studios Journal.

Is Genome suitable for experienced TCG players?

Genome is designed to reward strategic planning, adaptation, deck knowledge and tactical decision-making. Experienced TCG players may recognize familiar ideas such as card synergy, tempo, control and aggressive pressure, while the ecosystem and evolution systems create a distinct style of play.

Can new tabletop players learn Genome?

One goal of alpha testing is to make the rules, terminology and starter decks understandable to newer tabletop players without removing the strategic depth expected by experienced card game fans.

Will Genome have additional ecosystems and cards?

The current alpha version focuses on five starter ecosystems. Genome is designed around an expandable world of creatures, Genes, Afflictions, Climate effects and evolutionary combinations, although future content will depend on continued development and testing.

Who created Genome: Apex Shifting?

Genome: Apex Shifting was created by Rory Kelly and is being developed through GhostWrite Studios, an independent writing, game development and intellectual property studio.
