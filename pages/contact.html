How Do I Hire a Ghostwriter?

To hire a ghostwriter, send a message describing the kind of project you want to create, its intended audience, approximate length, current materials, preferred tone and desired outcome.

Useful source materials may include notes, outlines, interviews, transcripts, research, rough drafts or examples of your natural voice. You do not need to have a polished concept before making contact.

After reviewing the inquiry, GhostWrite Studios can determine whether the project is a good fit and discuss scope, availability, deliverables and pricing.

How Do I Hire an Editor?

To hire an editor, describe the type of project, its current word count or page count, the stage of development and the problems you want the edit to address.

Editing may include developmental editing, structural editing, line editing, copy editing, proofreading, dialogue refinement, screenplay editing, pacing analysis or AI content editing.

It is also useful to mention your deadline, intended audience and whether you want detailed editorial notes, direct changes to the text or both.

Review available editing and writing services .

How Do I Hire a Screenwriter or Screenplay Editor?

Send a short description of the film or television project, including the genre, format, current page count, development stage and the kind of help needed.

Screenwriting services may include original screenplay development, screenplay editing, dialogue revision, character development, story structure, pacing analysis, script coverage and creative consulting.

You may also include a logline, synopsis or brief explanation of the central concept. There is no need to submit an entire screenplay before the project and service have been discussed.

What Should I Include in a Writing Project Inquiry?

Include the project type, approximate length, current stage, intended audience, desired service, deadline and expected outcome.

You should also mention whether you already have an outline, draft, research, interviews, references or other source material. More context allows GhostWrite Studios to assess the work accurately instead of estimating through interpretive smoke signals.

How Much Do Ghostwriting and Editing Services Cost?

Pricing depends on the project type, length, complexity, research requirements, editing depth, deadline and condition of the existing material.

A short article, complete screenplay edit and book-length ghostwriting project require very different amounts of work. Submit the project details through the contact form so the scope can be reviewed before pricing is discussed.

Are Client Projects Kept Confidential?

Client materials, unpublished ideas and project details are treated as private professional information.

Any specific nondisclosure agreement, ownership requirement, credit arrangement or confidentiality term should be discussed before work begins.

Who Owns the Finished Work?

Ownership and usage rights depend on the type of service and the written agreement for the project.

Ghostwriting, editing, collaboration, screenwriting and intellectual property development may involve different credit and ownership arrangements. Those terms should be clearly established before the project begins.

Can GhostWrite Studios Work With an Existing Draft?

Yes. Projects may begin as rough notes, incomplete drafts, interview transcripts, outlines, finished manuscripts, screenplays or AI-assisted writing.

The condition of the material helps determine whether the project needs ghostwriting, rewriting, developmental editing, line editing, proofreading or another service.

Does GhostWrite Studios Edit AI-Assisted Writing?

Yes. AI content editing focuses on removing generic phrasing, repetitive structure, filler language, false confidence, invented details and other patterns that weaken AI-assisted writing.

The purpose is to create clearer, more accurate and more natural work while preserving the writer's intended meaning and individual voice.
