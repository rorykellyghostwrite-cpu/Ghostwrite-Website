Professional Ghostwriting

What Does a Ghostwriter Do?

A ghostwriter creates written work on behalf of another person, company or organization. The client provides the ideas, experience, message, source material or desired outcome, and the ghostwriter develops that information into polished content.

Ghostwriting may begin with an outline, interview, transcript, collection of notes, incomplete draft or little more than a clear concept.

The goal is not to replace the client's identity. The goal is to communicate that identity more clearly and effectively.

How Do I Hire a Ghostwriter?

Begin by describing the type of project, intended audience, approximate length, preferred tone, available source material, deadline and intended outcome.

You do not need to arrive with a complete outline. Useful material may include notes, interviews, recordings, research, examples of your natural voice or a rough explanation of what you want the finished project to accomplish.

Once the project has been reviewed, the scope, deliverables, timeline and pricing can be discussed.

Professional Editing

What Kind of Editor Do I Need?

The right editing service depends on the condition of the project and the problems that need to be solved.

Developmental Editing

Focuses on the entire project, including structure, organization, pacing, logic, character, argument and missing information.

Line Editing

Improves sentence flow, voice, rhythm, clarity, tone and word choice.

Copy Editing

Corrects grammar, punctuation, usage, consistency and mechanical errors.

Proofreading

Provides a final review for typographical errors, formatting problems and minor mistakes before publication.

Rewriting

Reconstructs weak or incomplete material when editing alone is not enough.

Screenplay Editing

Focuses on story structure, visual storytelling, scene construction, dialogue, pacing, character and screenplay format.

How Do I Hire an Editor?

Send the project type, current word count or page count, stage of development, deadline and the kind of help you believe the work needs.

It is also useful to explain whether you want direct edits, editorial notes, a full rewrite or a combination of approaches.

If you are uncertain which level of editing is appropriate, describe the problems you are encountering. The service can be determined after reviewing the project.

AI Content Editing

What Is AI Decontamination?

AI decontamination is the process of revising AI-assisted writing so it becomes clearer, more accurate, more natural and more consistent with the writer's actual voice.

This may involve removing repetitive phrasing, generic transitions, filler language, exaggerated claims, invented details, mechanical sentence patterns and unnecessary summaries.

The objective is not to disguise technology. It is to ensure the final work reflects human judgment, intention and responsibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Much Do Ghostwriting and Editing Services Cost?

Pricing depends on the type of project, length, complexity, research requirements, editing depth, deadline and condition of the existing material.

A short article, full screenplay rewrite and book-length ghostwriting project require very different amounts of work. Submit the project details before requesting an accurate quote.

Can You Work From an Existing Draft?

Yes. Projects may begin as notes, outlines, transcripts, partial drafts, completed manuscripts, screenplays or AI-assisted writing.

The condition of the material helps determine whether the project needs editing, rewriting, ghostwriting or another service.

Can You Help With Only Part of a Project?

Yes. Services may focus on a specific chapter, article, screenplay sequence, character arc, website page, dialogue pass or structural problem.

The scope should be clearly defined before work begins.

Will the Finished Work Still Sound Like Me?

Preserving the client's voice is a central goal of ghostwriting and editing.

Source material, interviews, writing samples and clear feedback help establish the intended tone and style.

Do You Keep Client Work Confidential?

Client materials, unpublished ideas and project details are treated as private professional information.

Specific nondisclosure, ownership or credit terms should be discussed before the project begins.

Who Owns the Finished Work?

Ownership and usage rights depend on the service and written agreement.

Ghostwriting, editing, collaboration, screenwriting and intellectual property development may involve different ownership or credit arrangements. Those terms should be established before work begins.

What Should I Include in My Project Inquiry?

Include the type of project, approximate length, intended audience, current stage, deadline, available source material, desired service and intended outcome.

A clear description makes it easier to evaluate the project without resorting to divination, which remains tragically unreliable for estimating page counts.
