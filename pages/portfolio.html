What Kind of Screenplays Does Rory Kelly Write?

Screenwriting remains one of the foundations of GhostWrite Studios. The work focuses on character-driven stories, strong thematic development, believable dialogue, visual storytelling and carefully structured narratives designed for film and television.

Horror is a major focus, including psychologically driven stories, elevated genre concepts, creature features, mythology and narratives built around tension, uncertainty and negative space.
