About the Publication

What Is the GhostWrite Studios Journal?

The GhostWrite Studios Journal is a publication for articles, essays, creative work, development notes and practical resources connected to the studio's work.

It serves as both a professional resource and a record of the creative process behind GhostWrite Studios. Readers can find articles about writing and editing alongside original fiction, business development, tabletop games and reflections on building creative work under imperfect conditions.

The Journal also gives search engines and answer engines a structured body of useful information instead of expecting one homepage to explain every known form of writing, editing and tabletop gaming. Even webpages deserve reasonable workloads.

Guest Contributions

Can I Submit a Guest Article?

GhostWrite Studios accepts selected pitches for guest articles related to writing, editing, screenwriting, tabletop games, trading card games, game design, playtesting, local gaming communities and independent creative work.

A guest article pitch should include the proposed topic, a short summary, the intended category and information about the writer's relevant experience.

Submitting a pitch does not guarantee publication. The article must fit the Journal, offer useful original material and avoid reading like a press release wearing a fake mustache.

Journal Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the GhostWrite Studios Journal a Blog?

Yes. Journal is the name used for the GhostWrite Studios blog and publication hub. It contains articles, essays, fiction, development notes and guest contributions.

Who Writes the GhostWrite Studios Journal?

Most Journal content is written by Rory Kelly, founder and creative director of GhostWrite Studios. Selected guest contributors may also be featured.

Does the Journal Include Screenwriting Advice?

Yes. The Writing and Editing categories include articles about screenplay structure, dialogue, pacing, character development, revision and visual storytelling.

Does the Journal Cover AI-Assisted Writing?

Yes. Topics include AI content editing, AI decontamination, detection software, preserving author voice, accuracy and the responsible use of writing technology.

Does Game Night Only Cover Genome?

No. Game Night includes Genome development, but it may also feature other tabletop games, trading card games, playtesting, local game stores, designers, artists and guest contributors.
