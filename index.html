A Studio Built Differently

Why Is GhostWrite Studios Building a Mobile Creative Office?

GhostWrite Studios is developing an off-grid creative office inside a 2002 Ford Explorer.

The project is designed to support screenwriting, editing, publishing, game development, remote client work and digital production without relying on a permanent office.

The build must also safely accommodate Baldur, a 55-pound dog, and Loki, a cat, while managing climate control, power, storage, workspace, internet access and daily living inside a compact vehicle.

Most digital nomad content shows the laptop after the problem has already been solved. GhostWrite Studios is documenting the wiring, layout failures, pet logistics, heat, storage, power calculations and other inconvenient details that usually disappear before the photograph is taken.

Intellectual Property Development

What Does an IP Development Studio Do?

An intellectual property development studio creates and expands original concepts that can grow across multiple forms of media.

The work may include worldbuilding, character development, screenwriting, game mechanics, story architecture, franchise planning, visual identity, lore, publishing and narrative design.

Instead of treating every screenplay, story, game or character as a disconnected product, IP development looks for the larger world, audience and long-term potential surrounding the idea.
