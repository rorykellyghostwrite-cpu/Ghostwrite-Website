Featured Studio Project

A creative headquarters inside a 2002 Ford Explorer

The mobile studio is not a decorative van-life backdrop. It is a working production environment being designed around professional creative work, limited space, off-grid power and the practical reality of traveling with a large dog and a cat.

The goal is a reliable place to write screenplays, edit client projects, publish journalism, develop Genome, conduct remote meetings and continue building the company without waiting for ideal circumstances or a permanent office lease.

Direct Answers

GhostWrite Studios at a glance

What is GhostWrite Studios?

GhostWrite Studios is an independent intellectual property development studio creating original entertainment projects and documenting the process of building the company publicly.

What projects is the studio creating?

The current slate includes original feature screenplays, Genome: Apex Shifting, the GhostWrite Mini Series, the Journal and an off-grid mobile creative headquarters.

What is Genome: Apex Shifting?

Genome is an original competitive tabletop card game about evolving creatures, changing ecosystems and biological adaptation.

Why build a mobile creative office?

The Explorer studio creates a reliable workspace for writing, editing, publishing and game development while traveling, working remotely and operating without a permanent headquarters.
